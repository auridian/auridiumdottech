Granite Docket

The problem

New Hampshire's legislation is one of the few parts of the public record available as machine-readable data. Many other records are PDFs, spread across different government sites. Following an issue means finding and connecting those sources.

What we built

One searchable site combining structured legislative data with records parsed from PDFs. Bills, legislators, hearings, court decisions, budgets, and other public records are linked together.

What changed

Granite Docket is used by legislators and political organizations. People can search across government records and follow the connections without piecing everything together themselves.
