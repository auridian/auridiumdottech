The problem

New Hampshire's legislation is one of the few parts of the public record available as machine-readable data. Many other records are PDFs, spread across different government sites. Following an issue means finding the documents, reading them, and connecting them to the people, meetings, and decisions involved.

What we built

We combine structured legislative data with records parsed from PDFs, bringing bills, legislators, hearings, votes, elections, court decisions, budgets, and more into one searchable site. You can find your representatives, follow a bill, and see when a public meeting is happening without starting over at a different site.

What changed

Granite Docket is used by legislators and political organizations. Information that was scattered across documents and pages is now connected in one place.

It's an independent civic tool, not an official government source.

What this means for your business

If we can reconcile the State of New Hampshire's data, we can reconcile your quoting spreadsheet with your accounting software. The scale is different. The job is the same.
