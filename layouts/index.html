How it works

More about how we work ↗

  1. A short call.

    Free, 30 minutes. You tell us what's slow. We tell you whether we can help, and if we can't, who might.

  2. A paid review.

    One day to one week, depending on the scope. We agree the fee with you first, then map the workflow and give you a written plan with a fixed-price quote for the first build. The review fee is credited toward it if you go ahead.

  3. A fixed-price build.

    Before work starts, we agree on what we'll deliver, how we'll check it works, and the price. You'll know exactly what you're getting.

  4. Coverage.

    For 60 days after handoff, anything we built that breaks, we fix. After that, optional monthly support, cancel anytime.

We'll recommend existing software when it fits and build custom software when you need it. We'll explain the setup and ongoing costs so you can make an informed choice.

“Can't I just do this with an AI subscription?”

Sometimes, yes. If a weekend and a subscription gets you what you need, do that.

We're for when it has to keep working: when the data from two systems doesn't agree, when the vendor changes something, when the person who set it up leaves. We're accountable for what we build, and someone answers when it breaks.
