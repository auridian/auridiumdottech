## Information this site collects

This website does not use behavioral analytics, advertising pixels, or dormant event-tracking hooks.

If you contact Auridium by email, your message and address are processed and retained by Auridium's email provider, Proton, so Auridium can respond and keep an ordinary business correspondence record.

If you use the contact form, you choose what to provide. The form asks for your name, email address, organization, goal, and current bottleneck. You may also provide systems involved, a successful outcome, timing, and a budget range.

## Tally and reCAPTCHA

The contact form is provided by Tally, a third-party form processor based in Belgium. Tally stores submitted form data in Europe and encrypts it in transit and at rest. On the current free plan, submissions remain in the Auridium Tally account until they are manually deleted.

The form uses Google reCAPTCHA to reduce automated abuse. Google may process device and interaction information as described in its own privacy terms.

Tally sends a submission notification to the Auridium-owned form account. Auridium does not add the form answers to analytics or send them to public AI services.

You can avoid the form and email [hello@auridium.tech](mailto:hello@auridium.tech) directly.

## Retention and requests

Auridium retains contact information only as long as it remains useful for the inquiry, an ongoing business relationship, ordinary recordkeeping, or a legal obligation. Tally submissions can be deleted manually; deleted Tally records remain recoverable in Tally's trash for up to 90 days before permanent deletion.

To request access, correction, or deletion of information you submitted, email [hello@auridium.tech](mailto:hello@auridium.tech).

## Service providers

- **Tally:** form hosting, validation, spam protection, and submission storage
- **Google reCAPTCHA:** automated-abuse prevention inside the Tally form
- **Proton:** email delivery and mailbox storage
- **Cloudflare:** website hosting, DNS, security, and network delivery

These providers process limited technical information needed to deliver their services. Auridium does not sell contact information.
