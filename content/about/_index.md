## Background

Milo studied mechanical engineering at MIT and has worked with early-stage companies across physical and software systems.

He is most useful where the problem is unusual, the existing systems are messy, and the solution requires moving across disciplines without losing sight of the actual operating objective.

## Clients work directly with the person mapping and building the system

Auridium keeps engagements lean and technically direct. The same person who studies the workflow, defines the architecture, and evaluates the tradeoffs also performs or closely controls the implementation.

### Reality before architecture

Understand how the work actually happens before choosing tools.

### Outcomes before code volume

Build the smallest system that materially improves the operation.

### Durability before dependency

Prefer systems the client can own, understand, and continue operating.
