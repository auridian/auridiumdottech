Auridium Technologies is an engineering company based in Manchester, New Hampshire, founded by Milo Hooper in 2024. Milo leads each project, bringing in specialist contractors when the work calls for it. You'll work directly with him from the first conversation through delivery.

Milo studied mechanical engineering at MIT, with a concentration in cyborg technology. His background includes physical product development and manufacturing automation, including work at a defense contractor. Auridium began with mechanical automation and process work for manufacturers and expanded into custom software and business automation.

That experience informs how we approach software: understanding the equipment, people, and processes it needs to work with. A scheduling tool has to reflect what happens on the shop floor. An integration has to account for how information is collected in the first place.

We also build and maintain Granite Docket, an independent site that makes New Hampshire's public records searchable and connected. It runs on infrastructure developed by Auridium.

## How we think about the work

- **Understand the work.** We look at the tools, handoffs, and decisions involved before recommending a change.
- **Use what fits.** When existing software meets the need, we'll recommend it.
- **Leave you in control.** You own the work, with documentation and a handoff so your team can run it.
