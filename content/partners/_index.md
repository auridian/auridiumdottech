## The one sentence

> "They automate the office work you're doing by hand, like quoting, follow-ups, and reporting, at a fixed price, and they're in New Hampshire."

That's it. That's the whole introduction.

## What happens after you introduce us

1. We arrange a free 30-minute call with your client by email.
2. If it's a fit, we propose a paid review with a fixed price. If it's not, we tell them so and, where we can, point them to someone who can help.
3. You get a short note from us letting you know how it went. We don't share anything about your client's business beyond that.

We don't sell them anything they don't need, and we won't make you look bad for the introduction.

## Signs a client is a fit

- Someone on staff spends hours a week retyping information between systems
- Quotes, invoices, or follow-ups go out late because they're done by hand
- The owner can't quickly see what's outstanding, who owns it, or what's late
- A critical spreadsheet or tool depends on one person
- They've said "we should really automate this" more than once
