Conducio is the platform we built to run our own work. It takes in messy information, structures it, and lets automated workflows act on it. It's what Granite Docket runs on, and it's part of the tooling behind some client projects.

It's private by design: data stays where the owner puts it, and it doesn't depend on any one AI vendor.

It's not currently offered as a standalone product. If you're a client, you'll never need to think about it. If you're curious about it for other reasons, email [hello@auridium.tech](mailto:hello@auridium.tech).
