## Flagship project: Conducio

Conducio is Auridium's flagship platform for private AI infrastructure.

It is built around semantic memory, workflow orchestration, tool integration, and agentic systems that help individuals and small teams operate with more autonomy and less cognitive overhead.

Conducio is also part of a larger thesis: the Sovereign Economic Agent stack. The long-term objective is to help technically ambitious individuals and small organizations coordinate knowledge, tools, agents, and workflows without surrendering control of their data or operating model.

## Systems that build systems

Conducio is not only a standalone product. It is also infrastructure Auridium uses to build and maintain additional tools, experiments, workflows, and client-facing systems.

This matters because our work is not just theoretical. We build the kind of automation and AI infrastructure we also use internally: memory-aware, tool-connected, privacy-conscious, and designed around actual operating constraints.

## Client and consulting work

Auridium's client work often involves private business systems, operational processes, prototypes, integrations, or engineering details that cannot be discussed publicly.

The public portfolio is therefore intentionally limited. If you are evaluating whether Auridium can help with a specific technical problem, the best path is to describe the problem directly and we can determine whether there is a fit.
